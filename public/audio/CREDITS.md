# Audio credits

The Japanese sound clips in `ja/` are synthesised speech, not recordings of a person.

- Speech engine: **Open JTalk** (Nagoya Institute of Technology), modified BSD license, used through **pyopenjtalk** (MIT).
- Voice: **HTS Voice "Mei"** © 2009–2013 Nagoya Institute of Technology, MMDAgent Project Team (http://www.mmdagent.jp/), licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The clips are derived from this voice (resampled, trimmed, normalised and encoded to MP3).
- Dictionary: Open JTalk dictionary (NAIST Japanese Dictionary, BSD license).

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/audio/build-audio.py` (see `docs/development/audio.md`).
